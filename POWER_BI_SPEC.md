## Recommended Management KPIs

**Fill Rate**

Units Shipped / Units Ordered

**Stockout Rate**

Orders with Stockout / Total Orders

**On-Time Rate**

On-Time Orders / Total Orders

**Profit Margin**

Profit / Revenue

---

## Business Insights to Highlight

1. Identify the strongest revenue and profit categories.
2. Identify suppliers with weak delivery performance.
3. Find warehouses with low fill rates.
4. Locate regions with high backorder exposure.
5. Compare shipping speed with on-time performance.
6. Track changes in revenue and profitability over time.
